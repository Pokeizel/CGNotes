Spheres

What's a sphere? ONLY THE MOST SOPHISTICATED SHAPE EVER CREATED BY HUMANS, AND...

Press to laugh.

It's what we use to decorate trees in compilers, too, I think.
Mathematically... spheres are defined by a center (x, y, z) and radius r (real, positive). For a point to exist in a sphere, the distance to the sphere's center must be exactly r. If points with a distance to the center equal or lower than r are chosen, then the object is no longer a sphere but a ball; spheres are hollow, balls are stuffed.

Things to look out for

Floating point errors are also implicitly part of the scene, so consider avoiding comparisons with exactly 0 and using a small range instead. Beware of type differences when calculating intersections... Usually scalars and vectors in different dimensions pop up all at once.
