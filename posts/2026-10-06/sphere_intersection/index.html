Equation for points in a sphere.


Expansion of the sphere equation substituting x, y, and z with the parametric forms of the eye position and direction.

Rearranging this a bit and simplifying, we reach the form t^2a + tb + c, where...

Parts of the new quadratic equation.

The discriminant (b^2 - 4c) gives us the following information: if it's negative, there are no intersections; if it's equal to 0, there's exactly 1 intersection; if it's positive, there are 2 intersections. This means t can have 0, 1, or 2 real solutions, which represent the distance to the intersection points with the sphere.
We now have most of the information we need to build our first basic raytracer!!!
