A Basic-A** Ray Tracer

This page doesn't cover slides or material seen in class, it's more of a personal note. You may skip it.
I made a ray tracer!! I started this before finding out it would be an official project, so I won't share the source code just yet. This was written in the OpenGL ES shading language, with GameMaker (a free 2D game engine, sadly not open-source...) only for managing input, window creation, and exporting.
My main area of interest around software is game dev. Despite computer graphics being arguably the backbone of video games, modern game engines have made it easier for anyone to make a game on their own, which is awesome, but in return caused graphics to be something developers tend to take for granted. Having knowledge of how engines and other software render scenes to the screen has been fascinating, it has made me much more excited to experiment with games and create more, despite robots wanting to take our jobs.
You can try the raytracer here. Use the arrow keys to move on the x and y axis, and use S and W to move on the z axis. Some funky stuff happens if you move the eye too far away from the projection pane. This may not work on mobile.

Thank you for reading!
