Previously, we saw some geometric transformations that can be applied to polygons: translation, scale, and rotation. Just like the scale operation, simply rotating a polygon does so around the origin, point (0, 0), which may not be what we want even if it does look kinda cool. To apply a rotation around an anchor, first translate the anchor to the origin, rotate, and translate the anchor back to where it was.
